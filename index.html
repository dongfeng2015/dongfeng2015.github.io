04 / HOW I WORK

Product judgment with an operator's respect for reality.

I'm most useful when the brief is still ambiguous, the system crosses functions, and someone needs to connect the intent to the implementation.

01

Start from truth

Research the market, inspect the current system, and separate the real constraint from the assumed one.

02

Design the states

Permissions, lifecycle rules, failure modes, and operational handoffs are product decisions — not cleanup.

03

Use AI as leverage

Break complex programs into clear contracts, route work deliberately, and keep judgment and evidence in the loop.

04

Demand receipts

Tests, audits, launch gates, and measurable outcomes turn confident storytelling into credible execution.

05

Fail loudly

An automated run that stalls visibly beats one that quietly invents its own version of the plan.

06

Say the tradeoff

Every recommendation names what it costs and who it disappoints. Unstated tradeoffs come back as incidents.
